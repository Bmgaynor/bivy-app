Bivy

Privacy policy

This policy covers the Bivy text message service described on the home page.

What Bivy collects

When you text Bivy, it receives:

  • your phone number,
  • the text of your message, including the coordinates you send.

Bivy doesn't ask for or collect anything else about you.

How it's used

Your phone number is used only to check that it's on the approved list and to send you the reply. Your coordinates and question are used only to answer that message.

Who it's shared with

Your phone number and other mobile information are never sold, and never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

To answer a message, Bivy uses these services:

  • Twilio delivers the text messages in both directions, so it handles your phone number and the message contents.
  • Open-Meteo receives the coordinates to look up the forecast. It doesn't receive your phone number.
  • Anthropic (Claude) receives your question and the forecast to write the reply. It doesn't receive your phone number.

Each processes the data only to provide its part of the service.

How long it's kept

Bivy doesn't keep a history of conversations. Its operational logs, which include your phone number and the length (not the text) of each message, are deleted automatically after 30 days. Twilio keeps its own message records under its privacy policy.

The composer app

The composer runs entirely on your phone. It reads your location only when you tap "Use my location", and it keeps Bivy's number, your saved questions and your last location in your browser's storage on that phone. It sends nothing to Bivy or anyone else; the only thing that leaves your phone is the text message you choose to send.

Your choices

Reply STOP to stop getting messages. To be removed from the approved list, or for any question about your data, email bivy@bradgaynor.com.
