Bivy

Messaging terms

The program

Bivy is a text message weather assistant. Approved users text their GPS coordinates and a question, and Bivy replies with a short answer based on the current forecast for that location. See the home page for how to opt in.

Message frequency

Bivy sends one reply for each message you send it, and never sends messages on its own. How many messages you get depends on how often you text it.

Costs

Bivy is free. Message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier or satellite messaging plan.

Stopping and help

Text STOP to opt out. You'll get one confirmation and no further messages. Text START to opt back in.

Text HELP for help, or email bivy@bradgaynor.com.

Forecasts are guidance only

Answers come from automated weather models and an AI assistant. They can be wrong or out of date. Use your own judgment and official sources, such as the National Weather Service and avalanche centers, when making safety decisions in the backcountry.

Delivery

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Satellite messages in particular can take minutes or longer to arrive.
