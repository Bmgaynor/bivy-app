Bivy

Backcountry weather answers by text message.

What it is

Bivy is a small personal weather assistant for hiking, climbing and camping. You text it your GPS coordinates and a question. It looks up the current forecast for that spot and texts back a short, plain answer. It's built to work over satellite SMS when you're out of cell range.

Send a message like:

47.6,-121.8 | wind and rain tonight?

and get a reply like:

Overnight: 2mph wind from the south, 3% rain chance. No rain expected.

Who can use it

Bivy is a private service for a small group of people who have asked to use it. Only phone numbers that have been added to its approved list get replies; messages from any other number are ignored.

How you opt in

To be added, ask the operator by email at bivy@bradgaynor.com. Once your number is approved, you start every conversation yourself: Bivy only ever sends a message in reply to one you sent it. It never sends marketing or messages you didn't ask for.

How often you'll get messages

One reply for each message you send. Message frequency depends entirely on how often you text it. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to opt out at any time, and you won't get any more messages. Reply HELP for help, or email bivy@bradgaynor.com.

Compose a message

The Bivy composer fills in your location, offers common questions, and opens Messages with the text ready to send. Add it to your home screen before a trip and it works with no signal.
